Everything you type in stays on this computer. Your customers, jobs, prices, photos and notes are in a file in the folder above and are never sent anywhere. JTS cannot see them and has no way to read them from here.

Three things do go out, and nothing else:

  • Anything you type in the Wishlist tab — that's the point of it, so the change reaches the person who builds this.
  • A check for updates, which asks "is there a newer version?" and sends nothing about you.
  • This app's own name, so the right update comes back.

If something goes wrong and you make a problem report, that file holds the error only — never your records — and it goes nowhere until you send it yourself.
